Open Positions

Big Dawgz Construction is looking for good people on both sides of the business, the folks who sell the work and the folks who build it. Here's what we're hiring for right now.

Project Manager

Our sales role. You'll meet homeowners after a storm, walk the roof, put together honest estimates, work alongside insurance adjusters, and see each job through from the first handshake to the final walkthrough. People skills and follow-through matter more than a resume.

Siding, Fascia & Window Installers

Experienced installers who do clean, careful work. If you've hung siding, run fascia, or set windows and you stand behind what you put up, there's a spot for you on our crews.

General Handyman

Anyone with solid general handyman experience. We do a lot more than roofs these days, remodels, repairs, outbuildings, and we need hands that can handle a little of everything.
